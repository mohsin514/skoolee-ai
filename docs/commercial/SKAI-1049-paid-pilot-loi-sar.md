**Letter of Intent: Paid Pilot of Skoolee Exam Studio**

Date: [DATE] · Reference: [LOI-REF]

1. **Purpose.** The parties intend to run a paid pilot of Skoolee Exam Studio alongside the School's existing systems.
2. **Scope (bounded).** The pilot covers at most two agreed campuses ([CAMPUS NAMES]), one grade and one term ([GRADE], [TERM DATES]), and one approved mathematics pack. Data is exchanged by import and export only. The pilot does not replace any SIS/LMS and has no connection to Noor, Madrasati or any government platform. Success criteria and the baseline method follow a pilot charter to be agreed (two complete cycles).
3. **Price.** **SAR [AMOUNT: FOUNDER-SET]** for the pilot term, [exclusive/inclusive] of VAT, invoiced [TERMS] and paid by bank transfer.
4. **Data processing.** No real student data will be processed until a data-processing agreement has been signed and the trust review referenced as [SKAI-1016 REVIEW REF] has passed. The School remains the controller of its data. At the end of the pilot, data is exported to the School and handled per the agreed retention terms.
5. **Non-binding.** This letter records intent only. It creates no legal obligation on either party except paragraphs 6 and 7. The binding obligations, including price, will arise only under a definitive pilot agreement signed by both parties.
6. **Confidentiality.** Each party will keep the other's non-public information confidential.
7. **Duration.** This letter lapses if no definitive agreement is signed by [DATE].

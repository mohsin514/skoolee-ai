## Recommendation and rationale

Keep all three markets in scope, but release curriculum packs sequentially after their own verification. Start Pakistan validation in Lahore and Islamabad/Rawalpindi; include Karachi as the Sindh comparison. Start KSA in Riyadh, then Jeddah; start UAE work in Dubai only. Prefer an accessible private-school decision maker and an academic champion over the largest institution. City sequencing is our operating recommendation, not a statistically measured ranking of purchase intent.
Use mathematics first, then one school-selected science subject, in a bounded secondary-school assessment cycle: approved source → reviewed paper/rubric → marking/moderation → released evidence → teacher action. Validate the same workflow alongside existing systems, rather than require ERP replacement. Board preparation is a proposed use case; never claim official board endorsement or certification.
Pakistan’s existing research ICP remains a hypothesis: owner-run groups with 2–10 campuses and 600–5,000 students. For KSA and Dubai, recruit private schools or small groups with a local budget approver; do not export the Pakistan size band as an established Gulf ICP. Initial implementation units should be one school, two campuses where available, one academic lead and two teachers—not an entire chain deployment.

## Edition evidence and unresolved details

- [FBISE implementation notice](https://www.fbise.edu.pk/notifications/NOTIFICATION%20of%20Implementation%20of%20Assessment%20Frameworks.pdf) names NCP 2022–23 frameworks, learning outcomes, model papers and tables of specification for SSC-II/HSSC-II. [IBCC MAF 2024](https://ibcc.edu.pk/model-assessment-framework/) covers grades 9–12. These support source-based mapping, but do not authorize copying SSC-II rules into grade 9 or treating NCP publication as board adoption. Verify the grade-specific current notice before pack approval.
- [BISE Lahore notices](https://biselahore.com/notifications) list changing 2025–27 schemes and 2026–28 registration cohorts. Its [grade-9 reduced syllabus for annual exam 2026](https://www.biselahore.com/downloads/notifications/misc/Rev_SmartSyllabus_9th%28ALP%29_2025.pdf) specifies a 75-mark mathematics paper. That is historical evidence of edition-sensitive paper rules, not a verified 2027 specification. Do not carry the reduced syllabus forward automatically.
- [BSEK’s official site](https://www.bsek.edu.pk/) exposes separate syllabus and model-paper resources. Karachi SSC must not be confused with BIEK HSC. No current BSEK pack is approved by this review.
- [Saudi MoE’s Ain guidance](https://www.moe.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/MOEnews/Pages/i-85976-t.aspx) identifies grade/term/subject textbook selection. This older guidance identifies a source channel, not the current edition. The first school’s academic owner must identify the exact approved materials and marking policy.
- [Cambridge 0580](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-igcse-mathematics-0580/) publishes distinct 2025–2027 and 2028–2030 syllabuses; the earlier revision includes a non-calculator paper. [Cambridge’s year rule](https://help.cambridgeinternational.org/hc/en-gb/articles/37190057441298-General-FAQ-Which-syllabus-year-should-I-use) uses the year the exam is taken. Store that separately from teaching year, country and language.

## Competition and buying hypothesis

Saudi [Noor](https://noor.moe.gov.sa/Noor/) already covers core learner records, attendance and examinations; [Madrasati](https://www.moe.gov.sa/en/knowledgecenter/eservices/pages/elearning.aspx) lists exams, assignments, resources and reports. [Classera](https://www.classera.com/en/products/classera-lms/) advertises generated exams and grading assistance. [Toddle](https://www.toddleapp.com/ai/) advertises assessment creation, grading and evidence-backed reporting. These are official product descriptions, not independent performance comparisons or proof of their adoption at a target school.
Therefore “AI exam generation” alone is not a defensible differentiation claim. Test whether versioned board/source evidence, reviewable marking, correction propagation and EN/UR/AR paper workflows remove a costly handoff that the school’s existing stack leaves unresolved. Interview both satisfied incumbent users and dissatisfied users; do not select only friendly schools. Stop or narrow if switching effort exceeds demonstrated value.
No reliable buyer willingness-to-pay evidence was found. Do not invent a regional price, discount, sales conversion or revenue forecast. D1 owns packaging/pricing; V3 owns payment evidence. Record local budget currency (PKR/SAR/AED), procurement owner, onboarding cost and support burden; currency formatting does not prove payment-provider readiness.

## One-page positioning for founder approval

**Audience:** private-school owners and academic leaders in Pakistan, KSA and Dubai who can sponsor a bounded mathematics assessment pilot alongside their existing systems.
**Problem hypothesis:** staff repeat work between approved curriculum, paper preparation, marking review and the next teaching action; errors and corrections lose context across those handoffs. This is not yet corroborated by buyers.
**Proposed promise:** Skoolee Exam Studio helps a teacher keep the approved source, reviewed assessment, marking evidence and follow-up action connected, with explicit human approval. Demonstrate only verified paths and label unfinished combinations unavailable.
**First offer:** one agreed school/campus scope, one grade and mathematics pack, a baseline cycle and a comparable follow-up; add one science subject only after educator review. A pilot agreement must separately define scope, cost, permissions, success measures and exit/export arrangements.
**Exclusions:** worldwide launch, other UAE emirates, all-board equivalence, official examination certification, guaranteed AI accuracy/learning gains/time savings, replacing Noor/Madrasati, unverified live SIS connectors, full ERP migration and automatic payment/provider commitments.
**Reason to continue:** official evidence shows addressable private-school segments and concrete curriculum differences worth testing; the existing assessment foundation makes a bounded comparison possible. **Reason to narrow/stop:** no independently repeated pain, refusal to pay, excessive correction/support cost, incompatible source rights or inability to satisfy school security requirements.
**Approval:** Mohsin Ali to accept or amend the cities, pack order and exclusions; review is pending. The original second-reader requirement is not marked passed merely because the founder offered to review. Record any founder-approved replacement of that requirement explicitly in Jira.

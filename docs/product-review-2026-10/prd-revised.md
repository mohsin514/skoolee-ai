Skoolee can compete through a fast, explainable, multilingual workflow that preserves context across those handoffs and works beside an existing SIS/LMS. ## One flagship with three memorable moments

1. **See what the evidence really says.** A teacher opens a reviewed class result and sees an item-level pattern, original response or scan, rubric and source. Missing, absent, unreadable and unmarked work remain separate. If the item is ambiguous or evidence is insufficient, propose a correction or a short clarifying check before recommending reteaching.
2. **Make the next ten minutes useful.** With available time, materials and learner context retained, AI proposes up to three practical options. It explains the evidence behind each and offers editable, temporary groups. The teacher approves one Learning Action, owned by SKO-425. Where two explanations fit, a small contrast check helps the teacher distinguish them. Do not infer a child's ability, disability or motivation from a score.
3. **Show what changed later.** A learner and family receive one released action; inability to help at home has an equivalent school-day path. A fresh, teacher-reviewed check later produces an observed improvement, no improvement or inconclusive result. Attendance at an activity and repeated-question success are not proof of transfer or causal learning gain. A portable receipt links evidence, teacher decision, delivery and follow-up.

The same assessment, learner mapping, objective and evidence versions survive every handoff. A correction makes dependent recommendations visibly stale. This continuity is more valuable than adding another disconnected AI tool. SKO-408 orchestrates; SKO-422 owns item interpretation, SKO-414 lesson activities, SKO-425 intervention ownership, SKO-419 family actions and SKO-426 practice. Keep one authoritative owner for each record.

## Product architecture and AI use

Sell the flagship as an independent institutional module with transparent capacity and service terms. Permit a school to import a roster and source material, run a useful cycle and export results without adopting fees, payroll, admissions or the whole ERP. CSV with stable identifiers and reconciliation is a valid first integration; advertise named live connectors only after end-to-end verification. School identity and roster exchange remain SKO-446; versioned APIs remain SKO-445.

AI belongs inside the task: source extraction; missing-information questions; objective mapping; editable paper/rubric drafts; independent answerability review; marking proposals; evidence summaries; feasible lesson alternatives; translated family drafts; and later comparison summaries. Deterministic services enforce scope, marks totals, versions, permissions, approvals, deadlines, idempotency and money. Models never become record authorities.

Evaluate by curriculum, subject, grade and language, including ambiguous items, contradictory sources, unreadable scans, prompt injection, absent learners and out-of-distribution work. Keep a held-out educator-reviewed set, model/prompt/policy versions, correction reasons and a rollback path. Wrong keys, cross-tenant disclosure and unauthorized actions block release regardless of average scores. Model scores do not prove answer correctness. All provider attempts, including failed and abandoned ones, count toward unit cost.

Use a budget per approved paper and completed Learning Action; cache only appropriately scoped approved artifacts; use deterministic validation before expensive generation; keep manual workflows working during provider failure. Track teacher correction time alongside AI cost so a cheap but unreliable model is not mistaken for a better product.

## Exam integrity and the actual lock boundary

The implementation target is a server-enforced Skoolee exam session. After Start, unrelated Skoolee pages, APIs and server actions are denied or redirected to the active attempt. Refresh, navigation, another Skoolee tab and a new login resume the same deadline and attempt. Only authoritative submission or server expiry releases this boundary. Logout remains possible for account safety but does not erase the active attempt. Device clock changes and local storage cannot unlock it. Save/submission ambiguity keeps the attempt in recovery rather than showing success.

A standard browser cannot prevent other applications, tabs, screenshots, developer tools, a second device or operating-system access. Managed-device lockdown remains proposed SKO-449 work with real-device admission and exit tests; it must not be advertised as shipped by a web-only change.

Where school policy enables camera supervision, preflight explains purpose, permitted viewers, retention, camera status and the school's accommodation path. Capture must be visible and permissioned. Camera denial, loss, device faults and upload failure are distinct conditions, with teacher recovery and preserved answers. Continuous camera availability cannot be guaranteed by a browser.

Record only bounded, relevant observations such as exam page hidden, focus returned, capture interrupted or a server-rejected action. These client signals are incomplete and can be spoofed; they are review leads. AI may summarize this timeline with event references and neutral wording for authorized teachers, administrators and principals. It must not infer cheating, emotion, gaze, identity or intent, assign a cheating probability, or change marks automatically. Review records show the candidate explanation, reviewer, disposition and appeal/remedy, separate from academic grading. Do not transmit exam video to a model without a separately approved, evaluated processing feature.

## Product outcome

SkooleeAI is a multilingual school-management and learning platform for standalone schools and school groups. It should help each role finish school work in a consistent, trustworthy interface, while reducing repetitive work through AI that gathers context, prepares drafts and executes only explicitly authorized, reviewed operations.

The flagship learning loop is: approved curriculum and source material → teacher-reviewed assessment → paper or standard-browser work → verified marks → approved result → useful student/family action → measured follow-up. School setup, academics, communications, fees, finance, staffing and operations use the same scope, review, recovery and audit principles.

A school joining for the first time must be guided from owner/institution identity through structure, academic year, classes, subjects, people, curriculum and optional opening balances. Setup is resumable and distinguishes confirmed facts, suggested defaults, unknowns and assigned follow-up. Existing and new work must preserve records and make reconciliation visible.

## AI interaction model

AI must lower the effort of school work without becoming an unchecked authority. The workspace supports text/chat, consented voice, upload, camera capture and scan where configured. It gathers missing facts with focused questions; shows what it understood; links extracted or generated content to source pages/regions and versions; makes uncertainty and unsupported claims visible; and lets the user correct drafts before saving.

AI may prepare structured drafts, summarize evidence and propose allowed actions. It must not invent school facts, silently alter authoritative records, publish marks/results, post/spend/reconcile money, send/broadcast communications, change timetables or apply consequential operations without the required current authorization and explicit review. Approved runs are bounded, versioned, expiring, auditable, idempotent and pausable. Permission, evidence or preview changes require fresh review. Manual workflows and saved drafts remain usable if AI, storage or a provider is unavailable. No raw sensitive prompt/audio or credentials in logs or tickets.

## Flagship assessment experience

Skoolee's flagship assessment loop turns approved curriculum into a teacher-reviewed paper, a focused learner attempt, verified marks and measurable follow-up. The product should feel like an educational co-author and a calm exam room, not a prompt box or a generic LMS form.

**Teacher and academic roles:** a teacher can start from chat, voice, a syllabus/textbook/past-paper upload or scan, correct extracted facts, answer only the missing setup questions, preview objectives/marks/duration/language, generate and edit questions, inspect each source and rubric, and approve an exact immutable version. Authorized academic staff control blueprint policy, release, accommodations and moderation. No draft or changed version is released without approval.

**Student role:** before starting, the student sees clear rules, timer/deadline, readiness and standard-browser limitations. After server-confirmed Start, the learner gets a focused exam-only Skoolee workspace with answer entry, question navigation, save state, approved accommodations and Submit. While the attempt is active, normal Skoolee pages and unrelated APIs are blocked. Refresh, back/forward, network interruption, reopening or private-window use resumes the same server-timed attempt; it does not release the app lock. Only final submission or server deadline expiry releases it. Server-confirmed answers and submission receipts are authoritative.

**Security boundary:** this standard-browser design controls Skoolee routes and actions only. A normal or private/incognito browser cannot prevent other tabs, apps, external websites, screenshots, developer tools or OS access. Never market it as a lockdown browser, treat focus loss as proof of misconduct, or change marks from focus events. Camera readiness may be required by explicit school policy before Start, with a documented approved alternative. Capture requires browser permission and visible consent. Loss or failure never changes marks or prevents saving/submission; staff review the context. Screen capture remains optional. Device lockdown requires the separately evaluated SKO-449 managed client/profile and deployment.

**Quality and inclusion:** source/objective traceability, deterministic coverage and mark checks, independent key-blind answerability review, teacher edits, immutable approved snapshots and candidate/key separation are required. English, Urdu and Arabic, RTL, mixed-script mathematics, accessible keyboard/screen-reader use, accommodations and printed output must be designed as first-class cases. Difficulty and parallel-form equivalence remain estimates until supported by moderated response data.

Delivery and ownership stay separated: [SKO-408](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-408>) owns source-grounded AI Exam Studio generation and review; [SKO-411](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-411>) owns server-timed attempts and the in-app session boundary; [SKO-407](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-407>) owns the bank/blueprint; [SKO-412](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-412>) owns scan-to-marks; [SKO-413](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-413>) owns schedule and invigilation.

## Definition of product readiness

* A new school can complete the intended setup and reconcile imported/confirmed facts without duplicates or hidden assumptions.
* Roles can perform their complete workflows without seeing another school's, campus's, pupil's or family's data.
* Assessment evidence, marks, reports, financial records and communications remain versioned, auditable and review-gated.
* AI outputs are source-grounded where applicable, editable, attributable and safely degraded; consequential actions obey server-side approvals.
* All routes and role surfaces use the shared UI patterns and are verified in supported locale, responsive, keyboard and error/loading states.
* Background work, retries, cancellation, receipts, restoration and operational monitoring are demonstrated.
* External integrations and real-world performance are labeled unverified until tested with approved providers and a school pilot.

# Skoolee AI — Consolidated Product PRD and Execution Charter

**PRD baseline:** 14 October 2026
**Roadmap structure:** Eight milestone projects on the Skoolee AI team board. This PRD is attached to the team, not to a milestone project. The 95 feature tickets are independent top-level issues grouped into the matching milestone project; they are not parent/child tasks. Each links directly to this PRD and carries its scope, acceptance criteria, repository baseline and verification checks. Blocking relationships show only true prerequisites.

## Product outcome

SkooleeAI is a multilingual school-management and learning platform for standalone schools and school groups. It should help each role finish school work in a consistent, trustworthy interface, while reducing repetitive work through AI that gathers context, prepares drafts and executes only explicitly authorized, reviewed operations.

The flagship learning loop is: approved curriculum and source material → teacher-reviewed assessment → paper or standard-browser work → verified marks → approved result → useful student/family action → measured follow-up. School setup, academics, communications, fees, finance, staffing and operations use the same scope, review, recovery and audit principles.

A school joining for the first time must be guided from owner/institution identity through structure, academic year, classes, subjects, people, curriculum and optional opening balances. Setup is resumable and distinguishes confirmed facts, suggested defaults, unknowns and assigned follow-up. Existing and new work must preserve records and make reconciliation visible.

## Users and authority

The product serves platform operators; school/group owners and administrators; principals; teachers; finance and operations staff; students; and guardians. Access is scoped by current identity, explicit membership, school, campus, class, student relationship, role and action permission. Names, email similarity or job titles never establish access. Each ticket must identify its actor, scope, authoritative record and permitted commit/review role.

## Product scope

The 95 sequenced feature tickets cover trust and shared UX; identity and membership; billing and recovery; school/group onboarding and records; localization and curriculum; admissions, staff, students and guardians; timetable and attendance; learning resources and question banks; AI-assisted assessment; assignments, exams, scanning, marking and results; student/family workflows; communication and action queues; insight/intervention; fees, payments, payroll and operations; country packages and integrations; low-bandwidth support; staged launch/advanced expansion; and the shared School AI operational-plan and reviewed-run lifecycle.

The feature catalogue is intentionally comprehensive. Priority and dependency order distinguish first-pilot work from later expansion; they do not imply every capability is already complete. Do not split acceptance into duplicate feature tickets. No separate QA issue is being created. The unique checks from the prior QA-only cards have been folded into the relevant feature issues; the user will create separate QA issues later.

## AI interaction model

AI must lower the effort of school work without becoming an unchecked authority. The workspace supports text/chat, consented voice, upload, camera capture and scan where configured. It gathers missing facts with focused questions; shows what it understood; links extracted or generated content to source pages/regions and versions; makes uncertainty and unsupported claims visible; and lets the user correct drafts before saving.

AI may prepare structured drafts, summarize evidence and propose allowed actions. It must not invent school facts, silently alter authoritative records, publish marks/results, post/spend/reconcile money, send/broadcast communications, change timetables or apply consequential operations without the required current authorization and explicit review. Approved runs are bounded, versioned, expiring, auditable, idempotent and pausable. Permission, evidence or preview changes require fresh review. Manual workflows and saved drafts remain usable if AI, storage or a provider is unavailable. No raw sensitive prompt/audio or credentials in logs or tickets.

## Shared UX and quality contract

All pages, dialogs, menus, tables, toasts, loaders, skeletons and role dashboards follow one shared design system and component ownership model. Use the existing restrained violet brand, neutral surfaces, readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, consistent actions and status language, and a responsive layout. Students and families need phone-first workflows; staff need efficient desktop review and keyboard access. Arabic/RTL and Urdu behavior, locale formatting, accessibility, reduced motion, and empty/loading/error/permission/success states are explicit acceptance surfaces. A shared component does not prove every route passed; attach route- and role-specific evidence.

## Assessment and exam boundary

Structured paper scanning stages data, shows original evidence beside proposals, validates roster/marks/duplicates, and requires teacher approval before import. Question/answer/rubric drafts remain private until approved. Results use exact versions and separate publication gates. The chosen deployment is **standard browser exams without device lockdown**. The web app cannot prevent operating-system app switching, another browser/tab or screenshots. Optional camera/screen evidence is separate, permissioned, consented, retained under policy and never proof of misconduct. Do not claim a normal browser is a secure lockdown browser.

## Role responsibilities and complete app-flow inventory

### Role boundaries and responsibility

| Role | Responsibilities and allowed actions | Approval and safety boundaries |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Platform operator | Provision and support tenants; manage platform plans, integrations and service operations. | Cross-tenant support requires a time-limited, scoped, approved and audited grant. |
| School/group owner | Establish school/group, campuses, administrators, institution settings and policy; act as purchaser where designated. | Must not assume access from name/email or silently override campus authority or record history. |
| Campus administrator | Manage authorized campus staff, students, admissions, classes, settings, attendance exceptions and operational records. | Sensitive access changes, imports, publication, finance exceptions and destructive actions use configured review. |
| Principal / academic lead | Own academic readiness, years/terms, curriculum/grading rules, timetables, exams, results/report review, performance and operational follow-up. | AI drafts, provisional grades, performance signals, exam schedules and reports remain unpublished until authorized approval. |
| Teacher | Work with assigned classes/subjects; record observed attendance; prepare lessons, assignments, questions, papers and marks; review evidence and contact authorized families. | No invented attendance/marks, no unassigned student access, and no unapproved publishing or correction. |
| Accountant / finance staff | Configure allowed fee schedules, issue statements/invoices, record receipts, reconcile payments, run reports and prepare payroll/accounting data. | Refunds, waivers, credits, reconciliation exceptions, payment corrections and outgoing reminders follow separation-of-duty and receipt rules. |
| Receptionist / admissions staff | Handle enquiries, visitor check-in/out, calls, postal records, complaints and authorized certificate requests. | Admission conversion and identity-sensitive changes require appropriate school review; no incidental access to grades or finance. |
| Librarian / operations staff | Manage catalogue, loans/returns, inventory and assigned transport, dormitory, facility, club or service tasks. | Safety, capacity, student handover and restricted health/safeguarding data stay within explicit scope. |
| Student | Access own timetable, attendance, coursework, assigned exam, feedback, released results and permitted fee information; submit own work. | Never another student’s record, answer key or unpublished result. |
| Parent / guardian | Access only currently linked children and permitted attendance, fees, schedule, reports, consent and communication actions. | Relationship and consent are checked for each child; school-private notes, unrelated children and staff-only drafts remain hidden. |
| AI coordinator | Gather context, ask questions, explain source evidence, prepare versioned drafts and call typed domain services. | It is not an authority or record owner; it cannot infer permission, silently mutate records, publish results or move money without explicit approval/policy. |

### Product workflow contract

For every role, the end-to-end contract is **identify actor and current school/campus/child scope → load authoritative records and permissions → collect missing facts → show source and confidence → prepare editable versioned draft → run deterministic domain checks → request the right approval → recheck permission and version → commit idempotently through the existing service → show receipt and audit/history → route follow-up**. Failed requests retain recoverable drafts; a queued message is not a delivery receipt; an AI answer is not approved school memory; a scan is never silently committed; current observed attendance and original marks remain immutable evidence with corrections recorded separately.

## Flagship assessment experience

Skoolee's flagship assessment loop turns approved curriculum into a teacher-reviewed paper, a focused learner attempt, verified marks and measurable follow-up. The product should feel like an educational co-author and a calm exam room, not a prompt box or a generic LMS form.

**Teacher and academic roles:** a teacher can start from chat, voice, a syllabus/textbook/past-paper upload or scan, correct extracted facts, answer only the missing setup questions, preview objectives/marks/duration/language, generate and edit questions, inspect each source and rubric, and approve an exact immutable version. Authorized academic staff control blueprint policy, release, accommodations and moderation. No draft or changed version is released without approval.

**Student role:** before starting, the student sees clear rules, timer/deadline, readiness and standard-browser limitations. After server-confirmed Start, the learner gets a focused exam-only Skoolee workspace with answer entry, question navigation, save state, approved accommodations and Submit. While the attempt is active, normal Skoolee pages and unrelated APIs are blocked. Refresh, back/forward, network interruption, reopening or private-window use resumes the same server-timed attempt; it does not release the app lock. Only final submission or server deadline expiry releases it. Server-confirmed answers and submission receipts are authoritative.

**Security boundary:** this standard-browser design controls Skoolee routes and actions only. A normal or private/incognito browser cannot prevent other tabs, apps, external websites, screenshots, developer tools or OS access. Never market it as a lockdown browser, treat focus loss as proof of misconduct, or change marks from focus events. Optional camera/screen capture is separately consented and permission-gated; it is never required for access or marks. A device-lockdown product requires a separately evaluated managed client and deployment.

**Quality and inclusion:** source/objective traceability, deterministic coverage and mark checks, independent key-blind answerability review, teacher edits, immutable approved snapshots and candidate/key separation are required. English, Urdu and Arabic, RTL, mixed-script mathematics, accessible keyboard/screen-reader use, accommodations and printed output must be designed as first-class cases. Difficulty and parallel-form equivalence remain estimates until supported by moderated response data.

Delivery and ownership stay separated: [SKO-408](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-408>) owns source-grounded AI Exam Studio generation and review; [SKO-411](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-411>) owns server-timed attempts and the in-app session boundary; [SKO-407](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-407>) owns the bank/blueprint; [SKO-412](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-412>) owns scan-to-marks; [SKO-413](<https://linear.app/skoolee-ai-app/issue/SKO-413>) owns schedule and invigilation.

## Definition of product readiness

* A new school can complete the intended setup and reconcile imported/confirmed facts without duplicates or hidden assumptions.
* Roles can perform their complete workflows without seeing another school's, campus's, pupil's or family's data.
* Assessment evidence, marks, reports, financial records and communications remain versioned, auditable and review-gated.
* AI outputs are source-grounded where applicable, editable, attributable and safely degraded; consequential actions obey server-side approvals.
* All routes and role surfaces use the shared UI patterns and are verified in supported locale, responsive, keyboard and error/loading states.
* Background work, retries, cancellation, receipts, restoration and operational monitoring are demonstrated.
* External integrations and real-world performance are labeled unverified until tested with approved providers and a school pilot.

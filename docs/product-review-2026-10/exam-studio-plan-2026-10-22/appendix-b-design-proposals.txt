##### ANGLE: ai-differentiation
THESIS: Exam season in a Pakistani or Gulf school runs on three instruments that do not talk: the office date sheet, the teacher's paper, and whatever marking happens on a weekend. Nobody knows before the sitting whether a paper is too long, too hard, untaught, or mistranslated; nobody knows during an online sitting which question the room is stuck on; after marking, the scripts carry the only evidence of what the class misunderstood, and that evidence is thrown away when marks are typed into the gradebook. Every AI product in this market generates questions or grades answers; none closes that loop, and most make claims (Bloom levels, "calibrated difficulty", "detected cheating") the school cannot defend to a parent or an inspector.

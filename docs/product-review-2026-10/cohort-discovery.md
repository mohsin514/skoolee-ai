## Scope and evidence baseline

The ticket recommends Pakistani owner-run groups with 2–10 campuses and 600–5,000
students, initially urban Punjab/Sindh and mathematics/science. These are recruitment
hypotheses, not a selected cohort or verified board support. Exact cities, boards,
syllabus editions, grades and subjects remain open founder decisions.

usage, willingness to pay, time savings or learning gains. Do not infer demand from
Exam Studio demonstrations or synthetic QA-01 test results.

## Consent script and handling

Before a session, the researcher names the research owner, purpose, private storage
location, permitted readers, retention/deletion date and withdrawal contact. Those
operational details must be agreed before collecting notes; this draft is not legal
approval or permission to process school data.

Suggested wording: “We are testing whether assessment work causes a problem worth
solving. Participation is optional; you may skip any question or stop. This is not
a sales or pilot commitment. May we keep anonymized notes for the stated research
purpose until the agreed date? We will not publish quotations or identify your
school without separate permission. Please use redacted or invented examples,
without student or guardian information.” Obtain a distinct opt-in for any recording;
default to no recording. Record consent scope/date and withdrawal instructions.

Keep contact/identity mappings and raw notes in approved restricted storage, never
in Git, public tickets or screenshots. Reference anonymous IDs and access-controlled
notes from the private decision evidence log. If consent is declined, record only
an anonymous refusal count; do not retain an interview account. Repository examples
must remain synthetic QA-01 material and never count as buyer evidence.

## Comparable buyer interview (30–40 minutes)

Ask the same core questions in every cohort; log source date and distinguish direct
observation, participant report and researcher inference.

1. Describe the last actual assessment cycle that was difficult. When did it happen,
   who did each step, and what happened next? Observe a redacted example if consented.
2. Which tools, paper handoffs and repeated entries were involved? How often does
   this recur; how much active work, rework and current expenditure does it require?
   Record units, currency, time window and uncertainty rather than invented savings.
3. Who approves budget, processing of school data and implementation? Who can veto?
   What procurement steps, budget cycle, purchase date and switching costs apply?
4. Before showing Exam Studio, which problem would you pay to remove first? What
   have you tried, rejected or stopped buying, and why?
5. Walk through the existing Exam Studio flow using synthetic QA-01 data. Compare
   it with that buyer-proposed problem. What is irrelevant, missing or worse than
   the current workaround? Do not assume AI is a reason to purchase.
6. What would you pay for this defined scope, in which currency, and from which
   budget? Compare institutional PKR plans and a standalone module priced in USD
   as hypotheses; record objections and no-payment answers. D1 and V3 remain open;
   an answer is not a price change, contract or forecast.
7. Name board/curriculum and exact edition/year, grade, subject, marking conventions,
   instructional and family languages, print needs, connectivity and devices.
   Name existing SIS/LMS, import/export needs, source rights, data-processing and
   onboarding/support constraints. Mark every unverified requirement unsupported
   or pending validation before any pilot proposal; a dropdown proves nothing.
8. Would you consider a separately scoped pilot? Record yes/no/undecided and reason,
   required approver, conditions and next decision date. Praise for a demo, intent
   to discuss and willingness to pay are distinct from an explicit commitment.

## Separate teacher usability session

Use the same synthetic QA-01 assessment and a participant-selected comparable task.
Observe task completion, active effort, corrections, confusion and recovery without
leading the participant. Include the required language and connectivity context.
Record the task/version, device, locale, assistance and failure conditions. A teacher's
successful session is neither budget approval nor proof of learning improvement.
EN/UR/AR and RTL product acceptance stays with the relevant implementation/QA owners;
this document does not certify those flows or change AI prompts/models.

## Private evidence record template (no real records in this repository)

- Anonymous session and independent-school/group ID; cohort, province/country,
  campus/student-size bands, role and session type; source date; researcher.
- Consent scope/date, approved note reference, recording choice, retention and
  withdrawal status; never expose the identity mapping in the analysis.
- Last incident, workflow steps/tools, recurrence/severity, cost/time units and
  evidence type; positive finding, negative finding and uncertainty.
- Budget, processing and implementation approvers; procurement duration/timing.
- Exam Studio versus buyer-proposed problem; exact curriculum/language/connectivity/
  SIS/LMS constraints; supported/pending/unsupported with verification reference.
- Payment response and conditions; pilot decision yes/no/undecided; distinguish
  demo interest, unpaid interest and explicit scoped commitment with evidence.
- Contradiction to current hypothesis; resulting analysis change or dated reason
  for exclusion; reviewer. Missing evidence stays missing, never zero or success.

Report completion/refusal by cohort and role, independent-school counts, recurring
problem frequency/severity, workaround costs and procurement duration. Report all
pilot decisions with yes/no/undecided denominators, dates and exclusions. Do not
collapse withdrawals, unavailable evidence or refused payment into positive signals.

# SKAI-904 — D4 LEAP role recommendation

Research cut-off: 29 October 2026. Owner/decider: Mohsin Ali. **For founder approval; not yet recorded in the PRD decision log.**
LEAP is the Riyadh technology conference referenced in `docs/SKOOLEE-AI-AUTOPILOT-ROADMAP.html` §11.

## Options considered

| Option | Description |
| --- | --- |
| A — commercial launch | Paid booth, public launch, Gulf pricing announced at LEAP 2027. |
| B — partner discovery + academic-only demo | Attend (visitor or startup zone), pre-book meetings with KSA school groups, distributors and EdTech partners; show a scripted Exam Studio demo on synthetic data. |

## Evidence, interpretation and limits

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [EventsEye LEAP 2027](https://www.eventseye.com/printable/p-leap-expo-26961-1.html) and [BERI](https://www.beri.net/events/leap-2027) (viewed 29 Oct 2026): 12–15 April 2027, Riyadh, 200,000+ attendees. | A large generalist tech audience; schools are a small slice. | Final dates — an [Export Virginia flyer](https://www.exportvirginia.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/LEAP%202027.pdf) says February 2027. |

| D2: KSA buyers already use Noor/Madrasati/Classera; no Gulf willingness-to-pay evidence; first KSA pack unverified. | Nothing is launchable for KSA yet. | — |

## Recommendation

**Partner discovery plus an academic-only demo (B).** No booth and no Gulf price announcement. Targets: 15 pre-booked meetings and 2 signed paid-pilot letters of intent from Riyadh/Jeddah private schools. The demo shows only the exam chain on synthetic data — no ERP, payments or student PII — and is labelled "pilot preview".

## Risks

- Dates move (February vs April) → confirm with the organiser (Tahaluf) by 30 November 2026.
- Demo over-promises the unverified KSA pack → "pilot preview" label and D5 gate.
- Low school density at a generalist event → pre-book meetings; do not rely on walk-ins.

## What it unblocks

D1 option B gate (two Gulf paid pilots), KSA pack prioritisation, `docs/leap/demo-runbook.md`.

# SKO-217 — permanent pupil identity and enrollment history

Pupil `Student.id` is immutable. Admission identifiers are institution-scoped, independent of placement roll numbers. `/pupils/[id]`, linked from roster cards, table IDs and the selection review, separates current placement from dated enrollment chronology. Registrars review the identity, effective date, preserved sources, guardian impact and target capacity, save a proposal, then explicitly confirm. Future proposals stay pending until their effective date. Stale proposals fail atomically.

Periods contain institution, campus, curriculum/board, class, year, roll, status and date-only boundaries. The initial admission is recorded by a database trigger, covering single and batch imports. The explicit policy is one non-overlapping enrollment per pupil: `[start, end)` ranges. Database locks, overlap checks and source-context triggers protect concurrent writes. Existing promotion routes use the same transactional transition. Direct roster class/roll edits require the review; bulk placement selection opens the individual reviews. Promotion preserves original invoices rather than creating duplicate receivables or summing different currencies.

Attendance, reports and invoices retain enrollment foreign keys and immutable period labels. Their date/class context cannot be reassigned. Invoice and payment PDFs, fee listings/aggregation and live report payloads use historical placement. Published report snapshots/bytes remain unchanged, and a placement roll change does not invalidate an old published report. Guardian links are explicit IDs and remain unchanged on transfer; a linked pupil login follows the new campus and its old sessions are revoked; campus-specific category, group, transport and dorm assignments are cleared on cross-campus transition for reassignment.

Families see only their own permitted history and published reports. Admission documents and proposals are restricted to registrars; private medical/contact fields are absent from the history response. Duplicate previews require institution administrator authority, inspect every student foreign-key dependency, and reject differing/missing guardian accounts, mismatched birth dates, pupil logins, family groups, cross-campus identities, or transactional dependencies requiring reconciliation. Compatible empty duplicate registrations become immutable, inactive aliases pointing to a surviving ID; originals/documents/timeline remain auditable. Names alone never consolidate or establish a family relationship.

## Migration and operating boundaries

Back up first. Apply the normal ordered migration set only in an approved deployment window. Rehearsal uses its own hard-coded loopback port; it does not accept a hosted `DATABASE_URL`. Fresh domain/browser fixtures expect a newly migrated disposable `sko217` database. Restore rehearsals expect unused `sko217_rehearsal`, `sko217_restore`, and `sko217_rollback` database names. Temporary databases and backups remain local until verification cleanup.

Legacy invoices never stored an original class; migration preserves their current known class as `LEGACY_INFERRED_CLASS`, visibly marked for verification. Historical attendance/report snapshots preserve available source class/campus; old roll labels can only reflect data that existed at migration. This does not retroactively reconstruct missing history or certify production data. Legacy source snapshots are explicitly distinguished from authoritative reviewed enrollment periods.

Target capacity remains a separately confirmed operational check because there is no class-capacity policy in the current class model. Concurrent enrollments are intentionally forbidden. Consolidation with existing academic/financial obligations is blocked for case-by-case reconciliation; it never bulk-reparents financial/report rows. Original related issues SKO-184 (group lists) and SKO-206 (student category/picture details) retain their own history/status; existing categories/groups/images are preserved within a campus and are not identity keys.

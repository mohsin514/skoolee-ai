## 2. Decision: attend, reduce or decline

The decision is made from costed assumptions, not from a guaranteed return. Exhibition attendance is an investment of money and founder time. Lead counts are not sales and are not investment.

Options to compare:

- **Proceed**: stand or meeting-room package, travel, accommodation, materials, and founder time.
- **Reduce**: attend as a visitor with pre-booked meetings only, no stand.
- **Decline**: spend the budget on direct school outreach or on the pilot.

Proceed only if all of the following hold at the base case:

1. At least the minimum number of qualified meetings is confirmed in advance (threshold set by the owner before the decision; not set in this file).
2. Total cost per qualified conversation is below the owner's stated ceiling.
3. Implementation capacity exists during and after the event to serve any school that signs.

If any condition fails, choose Reduce or Decline and record the reason.

## 4. Audiences and meeting goals

Each audience gets a different next step. Do not mix them in one sequence.

| Audience | Why relevant | Qualification criteria (all required to count as qualified) | Meeting goal | Next step if qualified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| School groups (buyers) | Could buy or renew the platform | Operates schools in the target segment; decision-maker or direct influencer identified; stated need that the platform covers | Agree a pilot scope or a demo follow-up | Pilot proposal, sent after authorisation |
| Implementation partners and distributors | Could deliver to schools | Existing school or education client base; capacity to onboard; agreed territory | Agree a partner kit review | Partner kit (see the distributor kit work, M12-02) |
| Investors | Could fund growth | Mandate covers education software in the region; stated interest in a follow-up | Agree a follow-up call date | Diligence index on request, after authorisation |

Rules:

- Target by professional relevance. Do not use broad mass lists.
- A contact who declines, or who did not consent, is not contacted again through an automated sequence.
- Investor conversations are recorded as discussions. They are not counted as revenue or as committed funding.

## 5. Lead register

A minimal register. One row per person and organisation pair. Store only professional contact details.

| Field | Purpose | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Name and role | Identify the decision-maker | Professional role only |
| Organisation and type | Segment: school, partner, investor, other | Required |
| Professional contact | Follow-up channel | Business email or business phone only. No personal numbers from event lists without consent |
| Consent and preferences | Record whether follow-up is allowed and on which channel | Required before any message. Default is no contact until consent is recorded |
| Need (one line) | Why the meeting matters | Free text, no student or guardian data |
| Scope | Pilot, partner kit, diligence, other | Choose one |
| Meeting status | Requested, accepted, held, not held | Counts feed the measures in section 7 |
| Outcome | One agreed next step, or one closed reason | Required once the meeting is held |
| Next action and date | Who does what, by when | Required when outcome is a next step |
| Retention | When the row is reviewed or deleted | Review 12 months after the event. Delete on request |

The register must not contain: student or guardian personal data, passwords, tokens, bank details, or identity numbers.

## 7. Measures and review

Report every measure with its denominator. Do not present interest as revenue.

| Measure | Definition | Denominator |
|---|---|---|
| Total actual event cost | Sum of invoices and receipts, by line in section 3 | Not applicable |
| Founder and operator time | Actual hours logged | Not applicable |
| Meetings requested, accepted, held | Counts from the register | Requested; accepted over requested; held over accepted |
| Qualified conversations | Held meetings that meet the section 4 criteria | Held meetings |
| Cost per qualified conversation | Total actual cost divided by qualified conversations | Qualified conversations |
| Consented follow-ups completed | Follow-ups done after consent | Consented contacts |
| Paid conversions and collected revenue | Contracts signed and cash collected from event-sourced contacts within the review window | Qualified conversations |

Review window: chosen by the owner before the event, and stated in the review. Recommended: 90 days after the event. Report signed contracts and collected cash separately from pipeline and unverified investment discussions.

Post-event review must separate:

1. Expenses (actual).
2. Qualified leads.
3. Signed contracts.
4. Collected cash.
5. Unverified investment discussions.

## Canonical schema invariant

`prisma/migrations/20261015200000_guardian_access_intervals/migration.sql` enforces that every
reader observes at most one permission version at an instant
(`guardian_access_versions_no_overlap`, an exclusion constraint over
`tsrange(effective_from, effective_until, '[)')` per relationship) and that an interval is
well-formed. This migration is part of the SKO-381 verified migration range and is present in
a freshly migrated database; it is the reason "granular access/consent" is interval-accurate
rather than a mutable latest-wins flag.

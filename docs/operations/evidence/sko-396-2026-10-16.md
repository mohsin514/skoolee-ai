newlines and a UTF-8 BOM intact, and that batch/ISO-date summaries are stable.

The School AI roster-import suite proves:
- Arabic and Urdu column headings map only to exact known fields and preserve quoted
  multilingual values (`علی، احمد`, `"12, Main Road"`, `معلوم نہیں`) without mangling them;

- import review tokens bind the exact reviewed selection and scope with canonical JSON
  ordering, so a changed selection cannot be smuggled past review (this is the reversibility
  guarantee: what was reviewed is what is committed, and nothing is written before review).

preview duplicates and correctable omissions → commit reviewed rows once) but requires the

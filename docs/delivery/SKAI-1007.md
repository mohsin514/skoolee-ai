# SKAI-1007 — approved pilot scope and capability eligibility

Decision record for D2-01. This document records the founder-approved scope and
the boundary that must be enforced when pilot capabilities are proposed or
activated.

## Approved scope

| Dimension | Approved value |
| --- | --- |
| Pakistan | Lahore and Islamabad/Rawalpindi; Karachi remains a comparison market |
| KSA | Riyadh first, then Jeddah |
| UAE | Dubai only |
| Pilot unit | One school, two campuses where available, one academic lead and two teachers |
| Pakistan recruitment hypothesis | 2–10 campuses and 600–5,000 pupils; this is a hypothesis, not a product limit |
| KSA / Dubai | A local budget approver is required; the Pakistan size band is not imported |

The approved scope is a positioning and pilot boundary. It does not certify
curriculum equivalence, official endorsement, AI accuracy, legal clearance,
payment readiness, or outcomes.

## Required decision record

- **Owner:** Founder / product decision owner
- **Decision date:** 28 October 2026
- **Rationale:** Start with a bounded, evidenceable market sequence while keeping
  geography independent from the actual capability contract.
- **Deferred:** Other UAE emirates, other countries, all-board equivalence,
  official endorsements, guaranteed AI accuracy or outcomes, live connectors,
  and payment-readiness claims without evidence.
- **Related planning ticket:** SKAI-902 (first cohort and boards)
- **Contract dependency:** SKAI-1008 (versioned curriculum contracts)
- **Capability metadata owner:** SKAI-958 and the approved pack for the selected
  board, grade, subject, exam year, academic year/term, tier and language.

## Eligibility boundary

Geography is never a capability selector. A proposal must carry independent
metadata for geography, board/qualification, grade, subject, exam year,
academic year/term, tier, language and approval status. The combination is
eligible only when the matching versioned capability record is current,
approved, and not revoked.

Unsupported, stale, unreviewed, or revoked combinations must fail closed for
both UI proposals and direct server calls. The response must explain the
reason and next action while preserving entered work. Published assessment
snapshots retain their historical version and are not rewritten by later
scope changes.

Second-reader review records discrepancies; founder approval records the
decision and does not stand in for independent QA. Any downstream handoff must
link this decision record and the relevant PRD evidence.

## Evidence and release boundary

Evidence for this ticket is the approved decision log and traceability matrix.
Eligibility allow/deny tests, direct-API denial, audit records, screenshots and
independent review belong with the implementation ticket that introduces the
versioned capability contract. Runtime activation remains blocked until the
SKAI-1008 contract and the relevant pack, trust and pilot gates are present.

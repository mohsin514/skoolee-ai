- **Series:** June and November.
- **Topics:** 53 Core topic codes and headings (C1.1-C9.5) and 71 Extended (E1.1-E9.7). These were checked line by line against both syllabus PDFs and are identical in both editions.
- **Year rule:** exam year picks the edition exactly. 2025-2027 and 2028-2030 are separate editions, and any other year is unavailable. The teaching year and the school's own grade label are stored separately.

## Content rights
- Only structure is stored: codes, headings and paper facts.
- No learning-outcome text, notes, examples, specimen papers or past papers are copied.
- Cambridge lets registered centres copy syllabus material for their own internal use. Skoolee is not a centre, so `rightsScope` is empty and publication is blocked until rights are recorded for each school.

## Rules enforced (`cambridge0580Errors`, `cambridge0580Availability`)
Rejects:
- an exam year that no edition covers
- a paper that does not match the tier (e.g. Core with P2)
- the wrong calculator rule, marks or duration
- sources from the other edition (mixed edition)
- source checksums or editions that differ from the pinned values (stale)
- outcomes outside the tier's topic list

When approved, `pinContract` pins the version and content hash, so older outputs keep their pin.

## Human-only (named `unverified` entries)
- **Paper sections:** the question structure for each paper, taken from the official specimen papers (structure only).
- **Rights:** rights scope and permission evidence for each school. Public access to the syllabus is not permission.
- **Grade mapping:** the school confirms which local year label sits each exam year and tier.
- **Local requirements:** the Dubai school confirms the pack is optional and does not replace mandatory local subjects. The KHDA requirements for the pilot year still need refreshing; 2024-25 guidance is not current approval.
- **Educator approval:** a qualified 0580 educator reviews a synthetic paper and rubric for each supported tier and paper, then sets approval and review dates.
- **Dubai budget approver:** recorded for the SKAI-1007 gate.

## Tests
`node --import tsx --test tests/school-ai/cambridge-0580-mathematics-pack.test.ts` covers:
- the DRAFT pack is gated off
- the facts for each paper
- the 2027/2028 edition boundary and unknown years
- rejection of wrong tier, paper, calculator, marks or outcome, and stale or mixed sources
- approval with test-only evidence, the pin, and the Dubai budget approver

## Excluded
Physics 0625, Pearson, CBSE, US and IB. A UK curriculum does not imply Cambridge.

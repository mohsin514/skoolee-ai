## Unverified (named evidence requests in the pack)
- **SLOs / outcomes.** The 2027 SSC-I Mathematics Assessment Framework is published on `fbise.edu.pk/curriculum_model_paper.php`. That page returned HTTP 403 to automated retrieval, so it must be obtained manually.
- **Paper format.** Total marks, duration, MCQ count, section and internal-choice rules, and the calculator rule all need the 2027 ToS and model paper. Model papers are structural references only.
- **Tier.** Science-group Mathematics vs Humanities Functional Mathematics (an FBISE notice dated 10 Aug 2026 was seen in search results but not checksummed).
- **Language.** The school's paper language (English or Urdu medium).
- **Rights.** Permission and rights scope for each pilot school.
- **Educator approval.** Educator approver, approval date, review date, and sign-off on a synthetic paper, rubric and coverage report.

No question bank, model-paper text or SLO text is ingested.

## Encoded rule
`fbisePatternErrors` rejects any FBISE paper from 2027 onwards whose MCQ marks are not exactly half the total.

## Tests
`node --import tsx --test tests/school-ai/fbise-g9-mathematics-pack.test.ts` covers:
- the shipped pack is blocked, names its evidence requests, and is ineligible
- source metadata is pinned
- a test-only placeholder completion can be approved and is eligible in Islamabad/Rawalpindi

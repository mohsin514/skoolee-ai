## Unverified (named evidence requests; human-only)
- **Textbook edition.** The school academic owner records the exact 1448 MoE edition as a `TEXTBOOK` source. ien.edu.sa failed TLS verification on 2026-10-29. Without a 1448 edition the capability stays unavailable.
- **Grade mapping and curriculum type.** The school confirms both.
- **Outcomes and assessment policy.** Total marks, duration, sections, choices, marking rules and the calculator rule.
- **Arabic notation.** A native Arabic maths educator confirms the notation and signs off a synthetic paper and rubric:
  - Arabic or Latin variables
  - Eastern Arabic or Western digits
  - the decimal separator
- **Rights.** Permitted source use for each source.
- **Educator approval.** Approver, approval date and review date, plus a separate review of the English assistance.

## Encoded rules
- `ksaPackErrors` rejects:
  - a wrong grade, semester or Hijri academic year
  - a foreign-curriculum school
  - a non-Arabic default language
  - grades outside 7-9
  - a missing or non-1448 textbook edition
- `ksaMathTextErrors` rejects bidi control characters in stored maths text. Direction is applied at render time, and the shared sanitiser already strips these characters.

No textbook, question or policy content is ingested. Noor and Madrasati are not replaced and no connector is claimed.
